# Realm Identity Matrix v0.2

**Status:** Accepted for construct audit  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-22  
**Scope:** Player-facing identity for the eight core realms

## Purpose

Realm ไม่ใช่คำตอบว่า “คุณชอบภูมิประเทศแบบไหน” และไม่ใช่ political faction ที่ผู้เล่นต้องเห็นด้วย แต่คือ **emotional worldview**—รูปแบบความหมายที่บอกว่าคุณเชื่อโดยธรรมชาติว่าอะไรทำให้ชีวิต คน และชุมชนดำเนินต่อไปได้

Politics อยู่ชั้นถัดลงมา มีหน้าที่แสดงให้เห็นว่า worldview นั้นสร้างสถาบัน ความงาม และปัญหาอะไรเมื่อถูกขยายเป็นทั้งสังคม

```text
Emotional worldview
        ↓
What earns belonging
        ↓
Healthy gift ↔ hardened shadow
        ↓
Culture and governance
        ↓
Story pressure
```

Realm identity ไม่ map ตรงกับ dimension ใด dimension หนึ่ง และห้ามใช้กฎแบบ `EXP สูง = Savanna` หรือ `ALG สูง = Arctic` ผล realm ต้องเกิดจาก configuration ของ animal candidates และ evidence หลายบริบท

## Realm profiles

### Taiga — The Boreal Dominion

- **Core question:** What must I make reliable before winter closes in?
- **Identity promise:** Your competence is not decoration; it becomes shelter, direction or continuity for something that matters.
- **Belonging currency:** responsibility carried well, steadiness under pressure, preparation that protects others
- **Healthy gift:** dependable action, foresight, endurance, quiet capability
- **When the gift hardens:** overfunctioning, emotional neglect, judging lives by output, refusing help because usefulness has become identity
- **Social atmosphere:** trust is earned slowly and expressed through responsibility more than display
- **Political consequence:** competence aristocracy and a council organized around survival functions
- **Sensory language:** dark pine, iron, fur, stored grain, fire seen through snowfall
- **Nearest-realm distinction:**
  - **vs Desert:** Taiga preserves enough to carry a responsibility through; Desert subtracts until only what is chosen remains.
  - **vs Alpine:** Taiga asks whether you can be relied upon; Alpine asks what you have mastered and become.
  - **vs Arctic:** Taiga respects what works; Arctic may keep a covenant even when efficiency argues otherwise.

### Arctic — The Ivory Expanse

- **Core question:** What promise remains binding when comfort and certainty disappear?
- **Identity promise:** Your loyalty makes survival communal; no one entrusted to the covenant should vanish into the white alone.
- **Belonging currency:** fidelity, ritual care, duty accepted despite personal cost
- **Healthy gift:** devotion, collective protection, moral continuity, reverence
- **When the gift hardens:** obedience without renewal, self-erasure, punishment of necessary dissent, fear of breaking inherited forms
- **Social atmosphere:** love is ceremonial, restrained and proven through what one refuses to abandon
- **Political consequence:** covenant law interpreted and embodied by a ceremonial keeper
- **Sensory language:** ivory, silver, breath, bells beneath ice, gestures repeated across generations
- **Nearest-realm distinction:**
  - **vs Taiga:** Taiga follows proven competence; Arctic follows sacred obligation.
  - **vs Wetland:** Arctic preserves a covenant by holding its form; Wetland preserves continuity by interpreting precedent.
  - **vs Rainforest:** Arctic belonging is explicit duty; Rainforest belonging is living reciprocity.

### Savanna — The Golden Crown

- **Core question:** What deserves to be witnessed, and will I stand where it can be seen?
- **Identity promise:** Your presence is not vanity; visibility can become courage, invitation and public momentum.
- **Belonging currency:** witnessed courage, charisma, contribution made legible to the community
- **Healthy gift:** confidence, social energy, bold accountability, the ability to rally attention around what matters
- **When the gift hardens:** status anxiety, spectacle without substance, humiliation as control, dependence on applause
- **Social atmosphere:** open, competitive and ceremonial; influence must be visible enough for others to answer it
- **Political consequence:** competitive monarchy shaped by reputation, patronage, public challenge and support
- **Sensory language:** open horizon, copper sun, red cloth, drums, dust and moving crowds
- **Nearest-realm distinction:**
  - **vs Alpine:** Savanna asks whether strength can move an audience; Alpine asks whether mastery survives the trial.
  - **vs Rainforest:** Savanna concentrates attention around visible figures; Rainforest distributes influence through relationships.
  - **vs Ocean:** Savanna makes identity legible; Ocean protects the freedom to remain changeable.

### Rainforest — The Verdant Empire

- **Core question:** What becomes possible when no life is treated as separate from the rest?
- **Identity promise:** Your way of connecting does more than create company; it allows people, ideas and possibilities to grow together.
- **Belonging currency:** reciprocity, participation, responsiveness to the living network
- **Healthy gift:** generativity, pluralism, relational intelligence, mutual adaptation
- **When the gift hardens:** enmeshment, informal coercion, hidden favors, inability to know where one obligation ends
- **Social atmosphere:** abundant, layered and intimate; influence travels through overlapping relationships rather than a single center
- **Political consequence:** a web of local courts, alliances, guilds and keepers held together through negotiated reciprocity
- **Sensory language:** jewel color, rain, canopy light, perfume, layered voices and paths reclaimed by growth
- **Nearest-realm distinction:**
  - **vs Ocean:** Rainforest adapts inside mutual dependence; Ocean adapts to preserve freedom of movement.
  - **vs Wetland:** Rainforest privileges living exchange; Wetland privileges memory and inherited context.
  - **vs Arctic:** Rainforest obligation emerges and changes through relationship; Arctic obligation is named and covenanted.

### Desert — The Sunless Crown

- **Core question:** What is worth carrying, wanting or owing when every excess has a cost?
- **Identity promise:** Your restraint is not emptiness; it is the ability to choose what deserves your finite attention, trust and desire.
- **Belonging currency:** discernment, clarity of exchange, self-command, respect for limits
- **Healthy gift:** sovereignty, patience, economy, commitment made deliberately
- **When the gift hardens:** chronic suspicion, emotional accounting, hoarding leverage, treating need as weakness
- **Social atmosphere:** deliberate, private and exact; generosity matters because its cost is never hidden
- **Political consequence:** power follows water, shelter, routes, storage and the contracts that govern access
- **Sensory language:** black cloth, copper, monumental shade, carved stone, water heard before it is seen
- **Nearest-realm distinction:**
  - **vs Taiga:** Taiga stores in order to sustain a shared responsibility; Desert limits in order to preserve choice and sovereignty.
  - **vs Alpine:** Alpine accumulates mastery through ascent; Desert reveals value through subtraction.
  - **vs Arctic:** Arctic accepts binding duty; Desert questions every debt before allowing it to become binding.

### Ocean — The Abyssal Court

- **Core question:** What can I become without surrendering the part of me that is still true?
- **Identity promise:** Your fluidity is not inconsistency; it is the freedom to meet change without letting one form become a prison.
- **Belonging currency:** adaptability, curiosity, depth, respect for movement and changing form
- **Healthy gift:** reinvention, mobility, tolerance for complexity, freedom from rigid identity
- **When the gift hardens:** evasion, unreliable attachment, romanticizing instability, leaving before change can become commitment
- **Social atmosphere:** mobile, mysterious and federated; authority and intimacy shift with currents, seasons and chosen depth
- **Political consequence:** federated currents whose coalitions and authority move with migration and circumstance
- **Sensory language:** pearl, black water, bioluminescence, distant song, pressure and light in motion
- **Nearest-realm distinction:**
  - **vs Rainforest:** Rainforest changes through ongoing reciprocity; Ocean preserves the right to change beyond any single network.
  - **vs Wetland:** Wetland keeps what remains beneath the surface; Ocean lets the current carry forms onward.
  - **vs Savanna:** Savanna stabilizes identity through witness; Ocean refuses to be fixed by one audience.

### Alpine — The High Kingdom

- **Core question:** What must I practice, endure or understand to reach the next ledge?
- **Identity promise:** Your ambition is not merely status; disciplined ascent can make you capable of seeing and carrying what your earlier self could not.
- **Belonging currency:** mastery, proof, disciplined growth, authority earned through practice
- **Healthy gift:** aspiration, craft, self-command, respect for earned competence
- **When the gift hardens:** conditional self-worth, contempt for limitation, isolation at the summit, interpreting misfortune as failure
- **Social atmosphere:** demanding, sparse and aspirational; recognition follows proof rather than performance alone
- **Political consequence:** meritocratic order governed by trials, mastery and the authority of the proven
- **Sensory language:** stone, thin air, flags, bells, monastery light and a horizon earned one step at a time
- **Nearest-realm distinction:**
  - **vs Taiga:** Taiga asks whether skill protects what already depends on you; Alpine asks whether discipline transforms what you can become.
  - **vs Savanna:** Alpine values proof even without an audience; Savanna turns witnessed presence into social force.
  - **vs Desert:** Alpine seeks the next capacity; Desert asks which desires are worth retaining at all.

### Wetland — The Drowned Court

- **Core question:** What does the present still owe to what has been submerged, forgotten or left unresolved?
- **Identity promise:** Your tolerance for ambiguity is not indecision; it lets you see histories, motives and continuities that simpler answers erase.
- **Belonging currency:** memory, interpretation, mediation, the ability to hold contradiction without premature closure
- **Healthy gift:** nuance, continuity, patience, restorative interpretation
- **When the gift hardens:** inherited grievance, endless qualification, manipulation through precedent, inability to release an old debt
- **Social atmosphere:** intimate, ancient and indirect; every gesture may carry a history that predates the people performing it
- **Political consequence:** legal continuity and patronage organized around precedent, old promise and negotiated exception
- **Sensory language:** dark green, black water, old gold, mist, candles and architecture half reclaimed by vines
- **Nearest-realm distinction:**
  - **vs Arctic:** Arctic asks whether the covenant was kept; Wetland asks what the covenant means after everything around it has changed.
  - **vs Rainforest:** Rainforest grows new connection through exchange; Wetland interprets the residue of connections already made.
  - **vs Ocean:** Ocean uses change to remain free; Wetland stays long enough to understand what change leaves behind.

## Shareability gate for realms

ก่อน realm ผ่านจาก `Review` เป็น `Locked` ต้องตอบได้ว่า:

- ผู้เล่นสามารถภูมิใจกับ identity line โดยไม่ต้องชอบ politics ของ realm หรือไม่
- share line อ่านแล้วสื่อ fantasy ได้โดยไม่ต้องรู้ lore หรือไม่
- gift มี prestige ชัดเจนโดยไม่ใช้ predator/status language เป็น default หรือไม่
- shadow เกิดจากรากเดียวกับ gift และไม่ทำให้ realm กลายเป็น good/evil หรือไม่
- silhouette, palette และ atmosphere ต่างจาก realm ที่ใกล้ที่สุดภายในสองวินาทีหรือไม่
- เมื่อเอาชื่อ biome ออก ยังบอกได้หรือไม่ว่าข้อความนี้เป็น realm ใด

## Scoring implications

Realm matrix นี้ยังไม่ใช่ scoring target โดยตรง ขั้นต่อไปต้องใช้ animal roster ตรวจ construct coverage:

1. Animal ทุกตัวต้อง embody และ/หรือต่อต้านบางส่วนของ realm identity อย่างเฉพาะเจาะจง
2. Animal คู่ใกล้กันต้องต่างด้วย core traits อย่างน้อยสองแกน หรือ candidate motive facet ที่วัดได้จริงหนึ่งแกน
3. Candidate facets เช่น recognition, mastery, continuity, reciprocity และ restraint ให้เก็บเป็น metadata ก่อน ห้ามเพิ่ม dimension เพียงเพื่อให้ครบหนึ่งแกนต่อ realm
4. หาก realm ต่างกันเฉพาะ lore แต่พฤติกรรมและแรงจูงใจที่คำถามมองเห็นเหมือนกัน ต้องแก้ construct หรือ question evidence ก่อนสร้าง vectors จำนวนมาก
5. Biome probability ต้อง derive จาก animal evidence โดย normalize prior ไม่ให้ realm ที่มี roster ใหญ่กว่าได้เปรียบโดยอัตโนมัติ
